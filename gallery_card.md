# Collective Echo
*Mercury Mine Monster*

---

## The Vision

A box that holds your voice.

Some things only land when said out loud — and even more so when echoed back through the voices of others who said them before you. This piece listens for what you say, weaves it into a growing chorus of everyone who has spoken into it tonight, and gives it back to you as a chord rather than a note.

What you put in stays in. Every echo from this moment forward carries a little of you in it.

---

## For Sandy

This piece is dedicated to **Sandy** — a dear friend who is in the hospital tonight and couldn't be here in person. Her voice has been recorded for the show. Calling her by name lets her answer back, and lets us all be in the room with her, even from far away.

---

## How It Works

The box is always listening.

1. **Speak** one of the phrases below near the mic.
2. The lights flash to acknowledge that it heard you.
3. Your voice is captured and mixed into the chorus of everyone who came before. Older voices recede; the newest sit forward.
4. The chorus plays back through the speaker, and the lights breathe along with it.
5. When the lights settle back into a soft glow, the box is ready to hear you again.

---

## What to Say

### *"I am a monster"*

The heart of the piece. **Mercury Mine Monster** is about claiming the parts of ourselves we usually hide — and finding out, when we say it out loud, that we are not alone in having them. Every voice that says it joins a chorus of others who have done the same. There is room for all of it.

### *"I love you"*

An extension of the same gesture, in the other direction. If "I am a monster" is the courage to be seen, "I love you" is the courage to see someone else. Speak it however you want — whispered, called out, soft, sharp. The chorus will carry your tone forward.

### *"I love you, Sandy"*

Calls Sandy's voice from her own small archive. Only her voice answers; what you say is held privately and not mixed into anyone else's chorus. A way to send love directly to her tonight.

---

*Take your time. Speak again if you'd like. The chorus grows with the night.*
